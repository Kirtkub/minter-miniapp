Deploy NFT Collection on TON

Tact contract (TEP-62 + TEP-66) with minting authorized via Ed25519 signature. Everything happens in your browser: no data leaves this page.

1 Authentication key

Generate the Ed25519 key pair the contract uses to authorize minting. The public key is stored in the contract at deploy time; the private key stays only here, in your browser.

Save the private key somewhere safe right away: your off-chain backend needs it to sign minting requests. If you leave this page, it will be lost.

2 Collection parameters

The URLs can stay at their defaults (still editable after deploy by simply updating the files on Netlify) or be customized now.

3 Connect your wallet

The connected wallet becomes the collection owner and the default royalty recipient. Scan the QR code with your TON wallet to connect it.

4 Compute address and deploy

The contract address is deterministic: it is computed locally from the code, the parameters and the public key, before any transaction is sent.
